Appeals

Every player deserves a fair hearing

What would you like to appeal?

🔨
Ban Appeal
You have been banned from the server and wish to contest the decision.
🔇
Mute Appeal
You have been muted in-game or on Discord and wish to contest the decision.

📜 Before You Appeal

  • Be honest – staff can see the full history of your actions.
  • Only submit one appeal at a time per punishment.
  • Spam or abusive appeals may result in further punishment.
  • Appeals are reviewed within 72 hours in most cases.
  • You will be notified via Discord once a decision is made.
